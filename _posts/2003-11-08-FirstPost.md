Something in me keeps asking: how did we see the beauty of this world and decide this was the only way to move through it? I've been sitting with that question for months. Swimming through a current that feels unknown, waiting for the ripple that'll carry me home.
And I guess I'm writing this as a form of solace. The feeling, I think, can find you at any age you're at.
In layman's terms, I've just been trying to figure out what's the next avenue for my life. I'm graduating, finishing a programme of studying and working that I started in 2022. The last two years have been hellish, and I've been feeling misaligned career-wise and also with the kind of life I currently move through because of my circumstances.
It's not been all bad. I recently came back from a trip to Spain for almost two weeks. It was nice to get away, feel the sun burning my forehead, and zigzag my way in the shades. So I'm thankful that I have the privilege of travelling. That was something impossible for me 4 years ago.
I've researched many "creative" corporate, high-paying careers I could go into, and even outside that, how I can actually express myself in various creative modes. I was getting a zeal for life again, but being back after being away, I've been slammed with this wave of hopelessness. I just want to lay down and do nothing, yet my mind is wandering about the CV refinements I have to make, I have to go to the office today, oh, I didn't get back to this person, and that parcel needs chasing. These all sound like regular life stuff, but for me, for my existence, it feels like I'm being put in danger. My nervous system just goes into flight mode.
